Questions

Preguntas frecuentes

Is this European Spanish or Latin American?

This edition teaches Castilian Spanish as spoken in Spain — with the distinctive pronunciation (the "c" and "z" sounds), vocabulary like "vosotros", and cultural context from the Iberian peninsula. A Latin American edition may follow.

How does it work — is it an app I download?

It is a single HTML file that works in any web browser — Safari, Chrome, Firefox. Open it on your iPhone, iPad, or computer. No app store, no installation, just open and go.

Will I lose my progress if I close the browser?

Your progress saves automatically in your browser. The app also has a backup feature — download a save file at any time and restore it on any device.

Is this suitable for complete beginners?

Absolutely. The Daily 10 starts with the most essential words and builds gradually. Grammar tips explain the rules as you go, including the famous ser vs estar distinction, with no prior knowledge assumed.

French and Portuguese editions available too?

Yes — French and Brazilian Portuguese editions are also available at the same price, each built to the same standard with the same features.
